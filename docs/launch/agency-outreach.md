# Agency & consultant outreach — the $6k→$10k lever

> The Teams/Agency tier ($18/mo · $149/yr) is the single lever that stretches the $6k base case to the $10k goal
> (ADR-0003, PLAN §6). Signing ~20–30 Agency-tier consultants is one of the three things that has to land. These
> people maintain **many client workspaces** and make the **same edits repeatedly** — the exact job saved recipes
> + multi-workspace management solve.
>
> **Framing rule: personal, researched, non-mass.** This is 1:1 outreach to a small, high-value segment — not a
> blast. If it reads like a mail-merge, it fails. Send ≤20–30/week, each genuinely personalized. Honest about what
> the tool is and isn't (no formulas/rollups; token stays on their device).

## Who to target & how to find them

Build a tracking sheet (crew can draft the columns; founder/crew fills prospects):
`name | business | workspace focus | how found | channel | personalization hook | touch # | last contact | status`

**Where they live:**
1. **Notion's official directory** — [PLACEHOLDER: notion.so consultants/partners directory URL]. Listed Notion
   consultants and certified partners; highest-intent, they do client work for a living.
2. **Template marketplaces** — Gumroad, Notion's template gallery, Prototion, Notionery, NotionEverything.
   Template sellers who also do setup/consulting. Filter for sellers with many templates or client testimonials.
3. **X / Twitter** — search "Notion consultant", "Notion setup", "Notion systems", "Notion for [niche]". Notion
   creators are very active here; a thoughtful reply/DM often beats email.
4. **LinkedIn** — title/keyword search "Notion Consultant", "Notion Expert", "Workspace Consultant"; agencies
   offering Notion implementation.
5. **YouTube / newsletters** — Notion productivity creators who mention client work; their audience is also
   template buyers (doubles as a creator-partnership channel).
6. **Upwork / Fiverr** — sellers with "Notion setup/migration/cleanup" gigs feel the bulk-edit pain acutely.

**Qualify before sending:** do they actually maintain *multiple* workspaces or resell templates? If they run one
personal workspace, they're a Pro prospect, not Agency — don't mis-pitch.

**Subject options (pick one, keep it specific):**
- `The find & replace Notion doesn't have — for your client workspaces`
- `Bulk-editing properties across [THEIR NICHE] client workspaces`

>

## 5-touch follow-up cadence (stop the moment they say no or after touch 5)

| Touch | When | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Email or DM | The personalized message above. |
| 2 | Day 4 | Same channel | Short bump + the demo GIF if you didn't send it: "Wanted to make sure this reached you — here's a 40-sec look. The recipe/multi-workspace part is the bit I think fits [their work]." |
| 3 | Day 9 | Alt channel (email→X, or vice-versa) | Value, not a nag: share one concrete recipe idea for *their* niche ("for a [CRM/wiki/client-portal] template, a re-tag recipe would…"). Offer a free extended Agency trial to evaluate on real client work. |
| 4 | Day 16 | Same as touch 1 | Social proof if you have it honestly: "[N] consultants are now using it for client cleanups" — only if true. Otherwise: a new capability or a question about their biggest repetitive edit. |
| 5 | Day 25 | Email | Polite close-out: "I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox — the door's open if the repetitive-edit pain ever bites. Either way, [genuine well-wish re: their work]." |

**Cadence rules:**
- Never send touch N+1 if they replied — switch to a real conversation.
- Every touch must add something (a demo, an idea, proof), never just "checking in."
- Offer a **genuine free trial of the Agency tier** to consultants who'll evaluate it on real client work — their
  feedback is worth more than the seat, and honest evaluation beats a hard sell.
- Cap total outreach volume so each message stays personal. This is a scalpel, not a blast.

---

## What NOT to do

- No scraped mass mail-merges, no "Dear Notion Consultant", no buying lists.
- No claiming multi-workspace magic we don't have, no implying we edit formulas/rollups.
- No fake "as seen in" / fake user counts. If we cite numbers, they're real.
