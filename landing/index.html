Privacy by architecture

Your workspace never touches our servers — because we don't have any.

Bulk Buddy is a client-side extension. It talks straight to Notion and nowhere else.

One destination only

The extension makes network calls to a single host, https://api.notion.com, and only for actions you start.

Your token stays local

Your Notion integration token is stored on your device in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). We never receive it.

No tracking, no selling

No analytics that exfiltrate your content, no ad identifiers, no data sold or shared. There's nothing to sell — the data never reaches us.

You stay in control

You decide which pages the integration can see inside Notion. Disconnect or uninstall clears your token from the browser instantly.
